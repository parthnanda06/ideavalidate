Problem Statement & Motivation

Addressing the high failure rate of early-stage startups through data-driven validation.

The Problem

90% of startups fail, often due to lack of market need. Founders rely on intuition rather than data, leading to wasted resources on unviable ideas.

The Solution

An automated, AI-driven validation framework that acts as a preliminary filter, providing objective scoring based on trained startup datasets.

The Outcome

Researchers and entrepreneurs receive a structured feasibility report, reducing risk and guiding pivot decisions before MVP development.

        About The Project

        Academic project developed for Final Year Project (FYP)

        Project Objective

        This system aims to bridge the gap between subjective ideation and objective market validation. By leveraging historical data and AI analysis, it provides early-stage founders with a "pre-market" feasibility check.

        Limitations

        The system provides probabilistic scores based on available data patterns. It does not guarantee market success or failure but serves as a risk-assessment tool.
